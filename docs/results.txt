[ascend-bench]   batch axis is validated by test_device_950pr_turboquant_multimode and not timed
[ascend-bench]   here. A GB/s column is that leg's traffic-model decode-read bytes over its own
[ascend-bench]   measured time, so it is what the kernel asked the memory system for and not a
[ascend-bench]   fill-rate ceiling.
[ascend-bench]   vs fp16 is against the physical fp16 Cube decode in this binary -- the same
[ascend-bench]   kernel with the codec removed -- so it isolates the codec. vs tq4 is the Cube
[ascend-bench]   against the vector cores at 4 bits, and since kv4fp8 became affine INT4 while
[ascend-bench]   tq4 stayed Lloyd-Max that ratio now moves two things at once; see the header.
[ascend-bench]   vs fia is against the stock CANN operator, which is what a caller has today.
[ascend-bench]   the kv4 decode is verified correct at S=64 on the camodel; these are the
[ascend-bench]   first timings ever taken of it, and at S in {512,1024,2048} its fidelity has
[ascend-bench]   not been measured anywhere. The fp16 column is a comparator with no fidelity
[ascend-bench]   check of its own. See TURBOQUANT_TESTS.md sections 13.8 and 13.9.
